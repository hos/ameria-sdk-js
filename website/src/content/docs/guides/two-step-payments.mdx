With two-step payments, `initPayment` only **approves** the amount — it is held on the
payer's card but not charged. You then deposit it with `confirmPayment` or release it with
`cancelPayment`. This suits orders you can only fulfil after checking stock.

<Code code={twoStep} lang="ts" title="two-step.ts" />

You can confirm less than the approved amount.

## Turning it on

Two-step is a setting on your API client, not a request option:

- **Test:** ask Ameriabank's technical support to switch your test client to two-step.
- **Production:** send an application through Ameriabank's mobile or internet banking.

:::caution
Once the bank switches a client to two-step, **every** payment on it is two-step. To offer
both kinds, ask the bank for a second client and create one `AmeriaClient` for each.
:::
