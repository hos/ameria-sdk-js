Saved cards — "binding transactions" in the bank's terms — let you charge a card again
without the payer entering it: subscriptions, recurring payments, one-click checkout.

## Turning it on

An authorised person at your company, usually the director, applies through Ameriabank's
mobile or internet banking. The feature works once the bank approves it.

## Saving and charging a card

<Code code={savedCards} lang="ts" title="saved-cards.ts" />

1. Pass your own customer ID as `cardHolderId` to `initPayment`. When that payment
   succeeds, the bank saves the card under that ID.
2. Charge it later with `makeBindingPayment` and the same ID. `paymentType` defaults to
   `"BindingMainRest"`.

## 3-D Secure on saved cards

The card issuer can still ask the payer to confirm a charge. Then the response has
`AcsUrl` (with `PaReq` and `TermUrl`) set and the payment is **not** complete until the
payer passes the check. Handle this case, or the charge silently never happens.

## Managing saved cards

`getBindings`, `deactivateBinding` and `activateBinding` list, disable and re-enable
saved cards. The bank only accepts `"MainRest"` and `"BindingMainRest"` as payment types
for these calls.
