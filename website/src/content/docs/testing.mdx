---
title: Test environment
description: How Ameriabank's vPOS sandbox differs from production.
---

Create the client with `isTest: true` to use `https://servicestest.ameriabank.am/VPOS`.

The bank sends test credentials, test cards and the details below by email when you
apply for vPOS. They are not published, and they differ between merchants.

- **Order IDs must come from your assigned range.** Each merchant gets a block of
  order IDs (for example `2350301`–`2350400`). An ID outside it, or one used before,
  is refused.
- **Only 10 AMD is accepted.** Other amounts fail in the sandbox
  (response code `560`).
- **Two-step payments and saved cards** must be switched on for your test client by
  the bank's support team before `confirmPayment` or `makeBindingPayment` work.

:::caution
These rules come from other integrators' reports of the bank's emailed guide (vPOS 3.1),
not from a public document. If the sandbox behaves differently for you, trust the
bank's email.
:::
